# Surface brief: Money Decision Engine app (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Platform: iOS first, shown in a browser device frame. Purpose: portfolio case study.
Audience and job: people in a considered moment (car, loan, prepay, safe spend) who need to see where they stand and what a choice changes. Constraints: keep all behavior, copy and calculations; theme and structure only.
Unresolved: dark appearance is a token set, not a full second design pass.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A money decision is a forecast, so the app speaks like a monsoon bulletin. Ranges instead of single futures, an onset marker where something changes, and a four-level warning vocabulary (be aware, be prepared, take action) instead of green up and red down. It refuses the category default of bone canvas, ink tiles and one neon accent.

OWN-WORLD: Overcast cloud-grey ground with a cool green bias, paper-white cards, a deep storm-teal shell for hero tiles, radar teal for actions, rain blues for data and estimates. The four warning colours (green, yellow, orange, red) are reserved for state and never decorate. Barlow Semi Condensed numerals over system San Francisco text, decimals muted. Contour art redrawn as isobars. Standard iOS tab bar, grouped lists and large titles.

STORY: The visitor sees where they stand, how wide the range of outcomes is, and when each choice changes the weather. They act by opening a decision, comparing it, and saving it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home. Large greeting title, a storm-teal net worth tile with a range-band sparkline and an onset marker, the income-flow ribbon, the emergency runway, then one bulletin card carrying a warning level and a plain sentence. Translucent tab bar at the bottom, Decide centred.

FORM: Monsoon bulletin, position 1 of 7 on the ordered list, chosen by the user over the railway route-line direction; seed: seed:8f533c6e1b2114b1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
